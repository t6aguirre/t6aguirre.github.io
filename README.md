# Tomás Aguirre - Personal Website

This is my personal academic website where I share my work in economics, artificial intelligence, and public policy.

## About Me

I'm a Research Scholar at the Centre for the Governance of AI (GovAI), working on the economics of frontier AI — particularly how AI's effects can be measured and what empirical infrastructure is needed to track them. I'm also a Research Affiliate at the Stanford Digital Economy Lab. I hold a BSc in Economics from the University of São Paulo (USP).

My research contributions include a chapter for NBER's Economics of Transformative AI volume and work on industry dynamics in frontier AI development in my paper "On Labs and Fabs."
